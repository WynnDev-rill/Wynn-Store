# Cinematic reference and asset research

Research date: 27 October 2026. Scope: upgrade the existing Astro documentary, using the supplied 44.7-second video as a quality benchmark. This is a curated comparison of four award-recognized relevant experiences, not a claim that there is an objective ranking of every website.

## Evidence and limits

The supplied video was sampled every four seconds into a contact sheet. It shows a dark portfolio: a luminous ring/black-hole focal point, warm architectural project spaces, a blue orbital sequence, large restrained typography and a consistent perimeter navigation. These frames establish composition, light hierarchy, spatial depth and sequence continuity. A contact sheet alone does not establish frame rate or the exact easing between frames. Do not copy the portfolio's identity, artwork or source code. Translate its visual discipline into the documentary's forest/peat subject.

The four references below were researched through retrieved Awwwards pages and creator case studies. Their descriptions and published engineering decisions were read; their complete running experiences were **not** browser-performance tested during this research. Weaknesses marked “tradeoff” are our implementation assessment, not measured failures in those sites.

## Quality criteria derived from the comparison

1. The first viewport has a deliberate focal point, a legible title, foreground occlusion and distant atmospheric depth; there is a complete initial composition before WebGL initializes.
2. Camera motion follows a bounded reversible path with stable horizon and time-based damping. Fast scroll, resize, anchor entry and reverse scroll do not lose narrative position.
3. Lighting belongs to the scene: cool ambient fill, a warmer motivated key and controlled bright highlights. Avoid blanket haze, washed-out text and gratuitous bloom.
4. Chapter transitions share typography, spacing and lighting grammar. A premium hero followed by unrelated card systems fails the coherence objective.
5. No forced intro, sound autoplay or scroll interception. Keyboard/touch controls, reduced motion, explicit pause, failed asset loads and unavailable/lost WebGL retain readable content.
6. Verify rendered desktop/mobile states and compare screenshots, not only build logs. Report emulation separately from physical-device performance. Keep source data, citations, map filters and their regressions in the test scope.

This research recommends visual principles and reusable assets; final implementation and observed test results are documented separately.
