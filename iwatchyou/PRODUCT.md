# IWatchYou

A private native Android app for noticing time spent in other apps. Success means
an advancing stopwatch above the focused app, with trustworthy local daily history.
Audience: the phone owner, including a Poco F6/HyperOS user. Language: Indonesian.

Preserve Accessibility setup, notification permission, monitoring toggle, automatic
focus tracking, Home/lock/PiP/IME exclusions, daily totals, weekly statistics, app ranking,
historical date selection, updater consent/verification, app/battery settings,
overlay compact/expanded/edge states, fading/drag and separate orientation positions. History stays private.
No new focus modes, goals, notification counters, accounts, network assets or polling.

User-approved reference direction: Digital Wellbeing App, Dribbble 11297277;
Mobile Widget animation, Dribbble 24002424. Adapt their visual language, never their
unrelated task-management features or copyrighted illustrations. Runtime remains Kotlin,
Compose Material 3 and a native Chronometer Accessibility overlay.

Owner revision: the overview shows at most five apps, with all apps and clickable
weekly history behind Lihat selengkapnya. Remove the hourly rhythm UI and repeated
helper copy. Edge hiding only hides the clock; tracking keeps running.
