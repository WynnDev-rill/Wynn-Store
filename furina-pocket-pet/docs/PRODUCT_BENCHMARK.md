# Companion quality benchmark

Reviewed 26 October 2026. This is a source/architecture comparison, not a measured performance ranking or a claim that these five were installed on a physical phone. Selection covers established desktop-pet behavior and relevant Android/Live2D implementations. Asset and code licenses are tracked separately in ASSET_AUDIT.md.

## Experience acceptance criteria

1. A pet reacts to a real cue: tap, fast drag, hop, wake or low battery. Dialogue must not invent what is on the user's screen. Local scripted text is labelled as local; AI is deferred.
2. Motion has continuity: bounded time-based paths, gaze changes, phased hops, fixed hip anchors for the small chibi foot adapter, and a stop/recovery state. Existing model motions take priority. No claim of a full animator-authored walking/sitting rig.
3. Comments remain occasional: 15-second global cooldown, urgent dizzy cue 5 seconds, same cue at least 35 seconds apart, no immediately repeated line; bubble expires after 6.5 seconds. User can disable comments and leg motion.
4. The pet does not take over the phone: only its small rectangular window receives touch; the separate comment window has FLAG_NOT_TOUCHABLE and opacity 0.8; hiding/pausing/screen-off/stop removes comments. Per-pixel input passthrough inside the pet rectangle is still not implemented.
5. Model identity survives interactions: position-only saves cannot replace a chosen model, stale async inspection cannot overwrite a newer model's displayed information. The owner APK contains the supplied ZIP; standard CI/public-source builds use the MIT fixture.
6. Verification is evidence-based: report compiler, unit, actual Android renderer/input/lifecycle and signing results separately. A host-side textured foot preview is not an Android personal-model rendering test. No physical Poco F6, FPS, RAM or battery benchmark is claimed without measurement.

## Remaining gap to a full desktop-pet animation library

The supplied VTuber model has original waving/expressions and separate leg meshes, but no authored side-view walk/run/sit cycle. The optional frontal foot adapter makes a limited chibi gait possible; it does not manufacture missing viewpoints, clothing folds or a complete sitting pose. Higher fidelity requires compatible authored assets or further carefully reviewed model-specific rig work. AI screen understanding remains explicitly deferred by the owner.
